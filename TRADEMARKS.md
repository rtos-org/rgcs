# RGCS trademark policy

"RGCS" and the RGCS logo are trademarks of RtoS Co., Ltd. This policy explains how you may use
them. It is separate from the software licence: the GNU AGPL lets you use, modify and share the
code, but it grants no rights to our names or logos (AGPL section 7(e)). The point of this policy
is simple: people should be able to tell the official RGCS from other versions.

## You may, without asking

- Say truthfully that something works with, is compatible with, is built on or was forked from
  RGCS: "Foo, based on RGCS", "Bar plugin for RGCS", "tested with RGCS 0.3".
- Redistribute **unmodified** official RGCS releases or source under the RGCS name.
- Use the name in articles, reviews, talks, teaching material and research papers about RGCS.
- Use the name and logo to link to the official RGCS project.

## Please do not

- Distribute a **modified** version, or offer one as a service, under the RGCS name or logo. Give
  it a different name and logo. You may still describe it as "based on RGCS". This includes
  builds with changed code, removed features or bundled extras, but not your own unpublished
  modifications.
- Suggest that RtoS Co., Ltd. made, endorses, certifies or supports your product, service,
  plugin or company when it does not.
- Use "RGCS", or a name easily confused with it, in your company, product, service or domain
  name, or as your social-media handle.
- Use a modified RGCS logo, or a logo easily confused with it.

## Questions and permission

If you are unsure, or would like permission for something not covered here (for example an
official partnership or a hosted service that uses the name), open an issue in this repository.
Security reports go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
