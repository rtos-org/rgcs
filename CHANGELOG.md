### Changed

  LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-02

- Initial public copy of RGCS: the Rust `rgcs-server` and web UI for ArduPilot vehicles
  (Copter, QuadPlane, Plane and Rover), with mission planning, WebRTC video, SIYI MT11 and
  MAVLink camera support, and map plugins.
